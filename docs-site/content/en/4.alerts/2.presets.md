Presets are created on install and on each migrate. Edit or disable them freely: they are never
overwritten. They are flagged **Dokos preset** on the rule. Read each condition as the rule's
aggregation over its window, checked every minute.

::warning
Presets have no channels. They open incidents but notify nobody until you add a
[channel](/alerts/channels).
::

## Web and deployments

| Preset | Fires when | Per | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 502 spike after deployment | `sum(http_502)` >= 10 in 5 min, after a deployment | `site` | ● critical |
| 5xx spike | `sum(http_5xx)` >= 25 in 5 min | `site` | ● critical |

## Cron, scheduler and workers

| Preset | Fires when | Per | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nightly cron failure (01:00-04:00) | `cron_failure` events >= 1 in 15 min, 01:00–04:00 | `site` | ● critical |
| Scheduler failure | `cron_failure` events >= 1 in 15 min, 04:00–01:00 | `site` | ◐ warning |
| Scheduler stalled | `host_metric_alert` / `scheduler_stalled` events >= 1 in 15 min | `site` | ● critical |
| Worker crash | `worker_failure` events >= 1 in 10 min | `bench` | ● critical |
| Worker queue backlog | `min(queue_length)` > 500 in 10 min | `host_label` | ◐ warning |
| Background jobs failing | `sum(rq_failed_jobs_added)` >= 5 in 10 min | `bench` | ◐ warning |
| Long-running background job | `min(rq_long_running_jobs)` > 0 in 15 min | `host_label` | ◐ warning |
| No background workers | `max(rq_workers)` < 1 in 5 min | `bench` | ● critical |
| Queue Redis memory high | `min(redis_used_memory_percent)` > 90 in 10 min | `bench` | ◐ warning |
| Queue Redis evicting keys | `sum(redis_evicted_keys)` >= 1 in 10 min | `bench` | ● critical |
| Bench process down | `min(bench_processes_down)` > 0 in 3 min | `bench` | ● critical |

## Sequences across sources

| Preset | Fires when | Per | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scan then login from the same IP | one of `web_attack_probe`, `network_ids_alert`, then one of `ssh_login_after_failures`, `ssh_root_login`, `new_admin_ip`, `admin_login_new_country`, `api_key_new_ip` on the same `ip` within 60 min | `ip` | ● critical |
| New-IP login then export or new key | one of `new_admin_ip`, `admin_login_new_country`, `api_key_new_ip`, then one of `data_exfiltration`, `api_key_generated`, `webhook_external` on the same `user` within 120 min | `user` | ● critical |
| IDS alert then risky change on the host | one of `network_ids_alert`, `hids_alert` (critical), then one of `server_script_change`, `client_script_change`, `console_execution`, `permission_change`, `security_settings_weakened`, `privileged_user`, `api_key_generated`, `webhook_external` on the same `host` within 60 min | `host` | ● critical |

**Per** is the rule's **Group By**: one incident per site, bench, host or `host_label` (a queue,
a domain). For a [sequence rule](/alerts/rules#sequence-rules) it is the entity: one incident
per IP, user or host. The conditions are those of a fresh install, generated from
[`dodock_wolf/wolf/presets.py`](https://github.com/bthuner/dodock-wolf/blob/main/dodock_wolf/wolf/presets.py)
by `docs-site/scripts/presets-table.py`. See [Security](/security) for the signals behind the
security presets.
